title=Tien's Generator Manager
description=Wire your generators. Right-click a generator, Generator > Manage Power, and paint the squares it reaches on a top-down map, one floor at a time.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Connect[/b] (green): this generator powers the square. [b]Disconnect[/b] (red): it does not. [b]Auto[/b] (yellow, default): any generator may.
description=[*]The closest running generator that has a square connected powers it; otherwise the closest one with it on auto.
description=[*][b]Blackouts:[/b] a square every generator in reach has disconnected goes dark. Lights, stoves, TVs, radios, washers, dryers and chargers there switch off, fridges and freezers are unplugged (food spoils normally, frozen food thaws), and everything that was on comes back when the square gets power again. See the caveats below for how this is done.
description=[*][b]Fair fuel:[/b] each generator only burns fuel for what it really powers, and the Generator Info window lists exactly that.
description=[*][b]See what is where:[/b] hover a square on the map to highlight its devices in the world, with a marker that shows through floors; right-click it for a picture of the square and the list of its devices. Zoom with the mouse wheel, right-drag to move the map.
description=[*][b]Show range[/b] tints every square the generator reaches, and [b]Show devices[/b] outlines every device in reach coloured by who powers it, through floors too (both in the Generator menu).
description=[*][b]Connect all, Disconnect all, Auto all[/b] set every square in reach on the floor you are looking at, with or without devices, at once. Squares you paint afterwards override it.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Caveats[/h2]
description=Project Zomboid has no power per square: a running generator powers every square in its reach and nothing can take that away from one square. So a blackout is imitated:
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Devices are switched off, not cut off.[/b] Switching one back on on a disconnected square works for a moment, then it switches off again within a second. The mod remembers it was wanted on and switches it on once the square has power again.
description=[*][b]Fridges and freezers have no switch,[/b] so they are unplugged by turning their shelves into a plain container: the loot window shows Fridge (no power) or Freezer (no power), food spoils at the normal rate and frozen food thaws. They become fridges again when the square has power. While unplugged, other mods that look for fridges do not see them. This can be turned off in the sandbox options (fridges then stay powered by whichever generator is in reach).
description=[*][b]Ceiling lights belong to a whole room:[/b] one switch turns every light of the room on or off. A room stays lit while any of its light switches is on a powered square, and goes dark only when all of them are disconnected. Lamps follow their own square. Lights also take power from the squares right next to them, as in vanilla, so the map also shows lamps and flood lights one tile past the generator's circle and one floor above it.
description=[*]Fuel pumps cannot be switched off, so they stay powered. Radios and TVs running on batteries are left alone.
description=[*]Switching happens on the server, where the area is loaded (a player nearby). Devices in an area nobody is near are switched when it loads again.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Adding it to a running save or server[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Safe to add mid-save.[/b] Every square starts on auto, so until you paint something every device keeps its power as before. The only change from the start: a device in reach of several running generators uses fuel from the closest one instead of all of them.
description=[*]Remove Nick's Generator Manager first (not compatible); its choices are not carried over. Generator Range is no longer needed either.
description=[*][b]Removing it:[/b] untick both sandbox options (Disconnected squares lose power, Unplug fridges and freezers) and visit every base once, so switched-off devices come back on and unplugged fridges become fridges again; then remove the mod. Removed without that, switched-off devices simply stay off until switched on by hand, but unplugged fridges stay plain containers for good (their food is kept). The map settings left on generators do nothing without the mod.
description=[/list]
